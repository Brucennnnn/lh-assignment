# Employee Leave Policy

Effective 1 January 2025. Applies to all permanent employees of LH Bank.

## Annual Leave Entitlement

Permanent employees are entitled to annual leave based on length of service:

- Less than 1 year of service: 6 working days, accrued at 0.5 days per completed month.
- 1 to 4 years of service: 12 working days per calendar year.
- 5 to 9 years of service: 15 working days per calendar year.
- 10 or more years of service: 18 working days per calendar year.

Annual leave is granted per calendar year and resets on 1 January.

## Carry Over

A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the following year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March. Days above the 5 day cap are forfeited and are not paid out, except on termination of employment.

## Requesting Leave

Annual leave must be requested in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance. Leave of 3 consecutive days or more requires approval from the department head in addition to the line manager. Requests are auto-escalated to the department head after 48 hours without a decision.

## Sick Leave

Employees may take up to 30 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave of 3 consecutive days or more. Sick leave cannot be carried over.

## Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave requires written approval from the department head and HR. It is granted only after all annual leave has been used.
