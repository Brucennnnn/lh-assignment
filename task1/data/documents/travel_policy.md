# Business Travel Policy

## Travel Expense Approval Process

All business travel must be pre-approved before booking:

1. Employee submits a Travel Request Form with estimated cost, dates, and business justification.
2. Line manager reviews and approves the business need.
3. Domestic travel with estimated cost up to 30,000 THB: line manager approval is final.
4. Domestic travel above 30,000 THB, and all international travel: additional approval by the department head.
5. International travel above 150,000 THB also requires Finance Director approval.
6. Once approved, bookings are made through the appointed corporate travel agent. Self-booked travel is reimbursed only up to the corporate agent's quoted fare.

After the trip, the employee submits an expense claim within 30 days following the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## Per Diem

- Domestic travel: 800 THB per day, covering meals and incidentals. No receipts required.
- International travel: 2,000 THB per day.

Per diem is not paid for days where meals are provided by the host or included in the conference fee.

## Accommodation and Flights

Economy class is standard for all flights. Business class requires department head approval and is permitted only for flights over 6 hours. Hotel caps are 3,500 THB per night domestic and 6,000 THB per night international.
