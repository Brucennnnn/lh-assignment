# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## How to Request Reimbursement

1. Submit the claim in the Finance portal under "Expense Claim".
2. Attach the original receipt or tax invoice for every line item.
3. Select the correct cost centre and expense category.
4. Submit for line manager approval.

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims older than 30 days require a written exception approved by the Finance Director and are rejected by default.

## Receipt Requirements

A valid receipt must show the vendor name, date, amount, and tax ID. Credit card slips alone are not accepted. For expenses under 500 THB where no receipt is available, an Expense Declaration Form signed by the line manager may be substituted, up to 2,000 THB per employee per month.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved claims are paid in the next payroll cycle. Claims approved after the 25th of the month are paid in the following cycle. Reimbursement is made to the employee's registered salary account only.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, hotel minibar charges, and personal travel upgrades are not reimbursable.
