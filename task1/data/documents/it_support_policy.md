# IT Support and Access Request Process

## Raising a Ticket

All IT issues are raised through the IT Service Desk portal. Phone support is available for Severity 1 incidents only.

## Severity and Response Targets

- Severity 1 (core banking system down, branch offline): response within 15 minutes, 24x7.
- Severity 2 (department blocked, no workaround): response within 2 hours during business hours.
- Severity 3 (individual user blocked): response within 1 business day.
- Severity 4 (request, question, low impact): response within 3 business days.

## Password Reset

Self-service password reset is available in the identity portal. Accounts lock after 5 failed attempts and unlock automatically after 30 minutes. Service Desk will reset a password only after identity verification with employee ID and line manager confirmation.

## Access Requests

Application access is requested through the Access Request Form and requires line manager approval. Access to customer data or production systems additionally requires Information Security approval and is granted for a maximum of 12 months, after which it must be recertified.

## Hardware

Standard laptop refresh cycle is 4 years. Replacement outside the cycle requires evidence of failure and department head approval. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the Service Desk immediately and to Information Security within 24 hours.
