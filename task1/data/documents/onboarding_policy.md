# New Employee Onboarding Checklist

## Before Day 1

HR sends the welcome pack and collects ID documents, tax forms, and bank account details. The line manager raises an IT Access Request so the laptop and accounts are ready on day 1.

## Day 1

A new employee should:

1. Report to HR reception at 09:00 for identity verification and contract signing.
2. Collect the laptop and access card from the IT Service Desk on floor 3.
3. Activate the corporate account and set a password in the identity portal.
4. Complete the Code of Conduct acknowledgement in the HR portal.

## First Week

1. Complete mandatory e-learning: Information Security Awareness, Anti-Money Laundering, and Personal Data Protection. All three must be completed within 7 days.
2. Meet the line manager to agree the 90 day objectives.
3. Enrol in the group health insurance scheme. Enrolment must be done within 30 days of the start date or the employee must wait for the next annual enrolment window.

## Probation

Standard probation is 119 days. The line manager must complete a probation review before day 100. Annual leave accrues during probation but may only be taken with department head approval.
