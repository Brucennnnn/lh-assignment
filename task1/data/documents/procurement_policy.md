# Procurement and Vendor Onboarding Process

## Purchase Request Thresholds

- Up to 50,000 THB: line manager approval, single quotation acceptable.
- 50,001 to 500,000 THB: department head approval, minimum 3 quotations required.
- Above 500,000 THB: Procurement Committee review and CFO approval, formal RFP required.

## Process Steps

1. Raise a Purchase Request (PR) in the Procurement system with specification and budget code.
2. Procurement validates the budget with Finance.
3. Collect quotations according to the threshold above.
4. Procurement issues the Purchase Order (PO). No work may start before a PO is issued.
5. On delivery, the requester confirms Goods Receipt in the system.
6. The vendor invoice is matched against the PO and Goods Receipt before payment.

## Vendor Onboarding

New vendors must pass due diligence before a PO can be issued: company registration documents, tax ID, bank account verification, and a sanctions screening check. Onboarding takes 5 to 10 working days. IT vendors additionally require an information security assessment.

## Emergency Purchases

Emergency purchases without a prior PO require written CFO approval within 3 working days after the fact. Repeated emergency purchases by the same cost centre are reported to the Audit Committee.
